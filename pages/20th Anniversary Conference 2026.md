<h2><strong>Reinventing the Tax Landscape: Get Future Ready! - 19 May 2026</strong></h2>
<hr>
<p><strong><a href="http://go.gov.sg/19may26-ta-conference" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Register Now!</a></strong>
</p>
<p>International tax rules are undergoing their most dramatic transformation
in decades. The tax landscape isn't just evolving—we're witnessing a complete
redefinition of how global taxation operates.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Join over 20 distinguished speakers from government, industry, academia
and the legal sector both local and overseas as they tackle the pivotal
issues reshaping Asia's tax future at this conference.</p>
<p></p>

&amp; National Development

Deputy Director of Centre for Tax Policy and Administration (CTPA) at the
OECD
<br>• Closing Keynote by Professor <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/john-vella-a3804a35/" class="ember-view" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">John Vella</a></strong>,
Director (Law), Centre for Business Taxation at Oxford University
<br>• Navigating Pillar Two: Mastering dispute resolution and tax certainty
<br>• The AI Revolution in international taxation
<br>• Global shifts and their ripple effects
